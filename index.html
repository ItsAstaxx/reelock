How it works — Lock re-encodes your video with FFmpeg.wasm at a bitrate and resolution calculated from your chosen settings, then wraps the encoded bytes with a small header and a byte-level scramble under a custom .rlck extension. If you set a passcode, the scramble key is derived from it, so the exact same passcode is needed to unlock. Nothing is uploaded — all processing happens on your device.
